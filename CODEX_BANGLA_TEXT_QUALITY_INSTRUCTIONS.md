Improve sentence construction, word choice, fluency and transitions, not just length. ## Sentence and paragraph rules

1. State the main point early, with natural Bangla subject/details/verb order.
2. Split nested claims where necessary; vary sentence length naturally.
3. Prefer exact verbs such as পাঠায়, গ্রহণ করে, বহন করে, শনাক্ত করে and সংরক্ষণ করে.
4. Replace stiff nominalizations and arbitrary English insertions where meaning allows.
5. Explain new technical terms before combining them; retain official names, acronyms and standard labels.
6. Use consistent terms: ডেটা, প্রেরক, প্রাপক, মাধ্যম, সংকেত, ব্যান্ডউইথ, নেটওয়ার্ক.
7. Avoid unclear pronouns, filler, repeated hedges and repeated full English terms.
8. Use logical connectors only when a real relation exists.
9. Give accurate, direct definitions in one or two sentences, followed by understandable explanation.
10. Preserve conditions that prevent false generalization. Keep necessary exceptions near their explanation.
11. A paragraph should develop one narrow idea: main claim, mechanism/reason, then example or implication as appropriate.
12. Use continuous prose for explanation; lists for actual enumeration, and tables for genuinely parallel comparisons.
13. Avoid motivational fluff, sensational claims, repetitive questions and fragment-heavy writing.
14. Preserve historical exam conventions as conditional conventions, not universal modern limits.
15. Retain the admission scenario without repeating it unnecessarily; retain all its useful applications and distinctions.
