## Continuation: permission terminology — 2026-10-03

The textbook's Index, Resource, Alteration and Drop authorization labels are preserved. A short qualifier now states that real DBMS permission names, levels and implementation can differ. This is an inference from [PostgreSQL 18: Privileges](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-priv.html), which associates modification and removal rights with ownership rather than treating them as independently granted privileges. The qualifier prevents the textbook taxonomy being presented as a universal DBMS organization; it adds no new permission syllabus.
