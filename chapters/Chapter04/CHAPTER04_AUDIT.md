## Overall findings

- Strengths: syllabus topics, extensive table practice, original Bengali explanations, definition styles, board-oriented revision, modern-versus-legacy warnings, DNS/client–server diagrams and corrected table diagrams already exist. Forms correctly distinguish POST from encryption and HTML from server-side storage.
- Missing/weak: no consolidated semantic-element guide; no explicit short successful-control submission example; accessibility advice is scattered; first definitions are repeated across introductory topics; code/output explanations seldom use a consistent “কেন এটি কাজ করল?” line.
- Repetition: 125 complete document examples, including all 76 table blocks. Many repeat the same `DOCTYPE/html/head/title/body` wrapper. Retain beginner context, complete-document lessons and complete projects, but shorten later examples to body fragments. Keep all unique table data, attributes, exercises and relevant head/body behavior.
- Layout: only the table-bank section and final MCQ bank currently use `multicols`. Most topic MCQs and short Q&A are stacked. Many small code/result pairs use successive full-width boxes. Several diagrams repeat a broad composition even when the figure itself is small.
- Language: generally fluent after an earlier pass, but occasional “উপরের”, “নিচের”, mixed English “output”, repeated introductory definitions, and phrases such as “বর্তমান মানে ব্যবহারযোগ্য নয়” need contextual cleanup. Source comments/unused fallback prompts are not student prose.
- Accuracy: preserve corrected nesting, optional-end-tag explanations, image dimensions, reset behavior and table cell counts. Qualify “সার্ভারের প্রক্রিয়াকরণ লাগে না” as no per-request page-generation requirement. Distinguish geometric table diagrams from pixel-identical browser screenshots. Label any retained presentation attributes beside the relevant examples.
- Density: retain 12pt body settings and readable code. Existing 8mm page margins already give a large text area; reducing font or margins is unnecessary. Baseline PDF indices with >140pt estimated lower unused area: 1, 18, 44, 63, 116, 125, 250–252, 255. Index 1 is contents and 255 is the chapter ending; those need no filler. The other candidates need composition changes, then renewed PDF inspection.
- Exam evidence: existing MCQ/CQ and labelled historical questions cover the core topics, but original question papers are absent. Do not invent/verify board/year attribution from memory. Preserve existing questions and identify attribution as inherited and unverified in source notes. Do not add facts solely to fill space.
